# Home Cave Interior Ideas

## Included now

- A plank-floored living area inside the main cave.
- A usable bed that restores health, hunger, and armor.
- Expandable storage chests for wood blocks and food.
- A crafting bench, forge, and glowing main cave gate.
- Craftable crystal lamps that create extra pools of light.
- Craftable tables, chairs, an echo rug, and display shelves.
- Five residents with conversations and repeatable missions.
- A craftable glow garden, map table, and echo monument.

## Ideas for future upgrades

1. **Trophy wall:** Display a unique trophy after completing each cave type.
2. **Crystal terrarium:** Grow cave mushrooms that produce food between expeditions.
3. **Armor stand:** Show the current armor tier and swap between cosmetic armor colors.
4. **Map table:** Reveal a small portion of the next selected cave before entering.
5. **Cooking hearth:** Combine two food supplies into a meal with a stronger hunger bonus.
6. **Music corner:** Craft stone chimes that change the Home Cave ambience.
7. **Pet nook:** Rescue a harmless cave creature that wanders around the interior.
8. **Alchemy shelf:** Turn mushrooms and crystal shards into temporary cave buffs.
9. **Wall banners:** Unlock a banner design for every five completed levels.
10. **Indoor garden:** Use flooded-cave water and fungal spores to grow decorative plants.
11. **Training dummy:** Practice blade strikes and lantern bursts without taking damage.
12. **Secret room:** Build a false shelf that opens a hidden treasure display chamber.
