# Cave Guide and Expansion Ideas

The game now includes the sixteen caves below alongside Basalt, Frost, Ember, and Verdant. Each cave changes one central rule while preserving the core loop: explore, manage hunger, collect crystals, survive monsters, and reach the awakened exit.

The current playable mechanics are summarized in the README. The more ambitious atmosphere, rule, hazard, resource, and objective notes below are longer-term expansion ideas; not every behavior described here is implemented yet.

## Flooded Grotto

- **Atmosphere:** Half-submerged ruins, blue-green reflections, dripping ceilings, and scattered air bubbles.
- **Core rule:** Shallow water slows ordinary movement. Sprinting crosses it more quickly but spends hunger at the sprint rate.
- **Hazards:** Deep-water tiles rise every set number of turns and temporarily block routes. Electric wisps can charge connected water tiles.
- **Resources:** Air pockets reveal safe routes, while floating supply crates contain more food than usual.
- **Objective:** Activate two drainage wheels before collecting the final crystal and reaching the exit.

## Crystal Cathedral

- **Atmosphere:** Tall crystal columns, bright colored light, and large chambers connected by narrow passages.
- **Core rule:** Lantern bursts bounce in straight lines between reflective crystal walls.
- **Hazards:** Reflected bursts can awaken dormant monsters, and unstable crystals shatter into dangerous floor tiles.
- **Resources:** Rare prism shards are worth extra currency at the forge.
- **Objective:** Aim reflected light into sealed crystal locks to open the exit chamber.

## Fungal Hollow

- **Atmosphere:** Thick moss, giant mushrooms, drifting spores, and pools of bioluminescent color.
- **Core rule:** Stepping beside glowcaps reveals a wider area for several turns.
- **Hazards:** Spore clouds increase hunger use until the player reaches clean air. Some mushrooms release crawlers when disturbed.
- **Resources:** Safe mushrooms become food; suspicious mushrooms may heal hunger or cause a temporary penalty.
- **Objective:** Find and cleanse three infected growths before the cave's heart opens the exit.

## Magma Veins

- **Atmosphere:** Black rock split by orange cracks, falling embers, smoke, and unstable bridges.
- **Core rule:** Lava advances into marked tiles on a visible countdown, forcing route planning.
- **Hazards:** Heat zones accelerate food spoilage, and eruptions briefly close passages.
- **Resources:** Cooling crystals create temporary safe platforms over lava and can freeze one eruption timer.
- **Objective:** Carry a cooling crystal to the central forge, then escape through the stabilized tunnel.

## Frozen Depths

- **Atmosphere:** Pale ice, frozen waterfalls, snow dust, and blue light trapped beneath the floor.
- **Core rule:** Ice tiles make the player slide in one direction until reaching rock or packed snow.
- **Hazards:** Frozen monsters awaken when lantern light reaches them, and cracked ice breaks after repeated crossings.
- **Resources:** Lantern bursts melt ice barriers and reveal preserved food caches.
- **Objective:** Recover heat stones that keep the final exit from freezing shut.

## Echo Labyrinth

- **Atmosphere:** Nearly total darkness, distant noises, sound ripples, and walls visible only in fragments.
- **Core rule:** The map beyond the lantern is hidden. A burst briefly outlines nearby walls, items, and enemies.
- **Hazards:** Stalkers hear sprinting from farther away, giving the sprint mechanic an important risk.
- **Resources:** Echo shrines provide one silent burst that reveals terrain without attracting monsters.
- **Objective:** Follow a sequence of echo tones to locate an exit that does not appear on the map.

## Ancient Mine

- **Atmosphere:** Broken rails, timber supports, abandoned machinery, hanging lamps, and collapsed work rooms.
- **Core rule:** Mine carts travel along fixed tracks and rapidly transport the player between distant chambers.
- **Hazards:** Weak supports collapse after the player passes, permanently changing routes. Moving carts can also carry monsters.
- **Resources:** Locked supply rooms contain food, shards, or a temporary tool upgrade.
- **Objective:** Repair three track switches and ride a loaded cart through the sealed exit gate.

## Living Cavern

- **Atmosphere:** Pulsing walls, root-like veins, breathing tunnels, and organic chambers.
- **Core rule:** The cave grows new walls after a countdown, gradually reshaping the route.
- **Hazards:** Tendrils grab nearby creatures, while closed passages can divide the map into isolated regions.
- **Resources:** Destroying growth nodes pauses all wall changes and drops healing sap.
- **Objective:** Defeat the central heart node before it completely seals the exit.

## Void Rift

- **Atmosphere:** Broken islands of stone, star-like darkness, purple energy, and impossible geometry.
- **Core rule:** Paired rifts teleport the player, monsters, and lantern bursts between distant tiles.
- **Hazards:** Some rifts change destinations after use, and shadow copies repeat the player's recent movements.
- **Resources:** Stable anchors lock one rift pair in place and can create shortcuts to isolated crystals.
- **Objective:** Align three rift anchors so that the final portal leads to the exit instead of deeper into the void.

## The Nest

- **Atmosphere:** Webbed tunnels, egg chambers, discarded bones, and constant movement in the walls.
- **Core rule:** Eggs display countdowns and hatch unless destroyed, creating a clear time-versus-resource decision.
- **Hazards:** Destroying an egg makes noise and draws nearby monsters. Sprinting helps reach eggs but burns supplies faster.
- **Resources:** Guarded survivor packs contain food and powerful single-use lantern charges.
- **Objective:** Destroy enough brood clusters to weaken the guardian, then defeat it to unlock the exit.

## Geode Garden

- **Atmosphere:** Violet stone gardens filled with crystal blooms and glittering deposits.
- **Playable rule:** Geode blocks take the place of ordinary rubble and release three shards when broken.
- **Future objective:** Find three resonant geodes whose tones open a sealed treasure chamber.

## Sunken Vault

- **Atmosphere:** Mossy metal doors, flooded machinery, rails, and abandoned lockboxes.
- **Playable rule:** Fast rails cross the cave, supply caches replenish expeditions, and ore blocks yield shards.
- **Future objective:** Recover numbered keys and open the vault rooms in the correct order.

## Glowstone Reef

- **Atmosphere:** Turquoise mineral pools, coral-like stone, and softly pulsing light.
- **Playable rule:** Standing in a glow pool expands the lantern's visible radius.
- **Future objective:** Redirect several light streams through the reef to awaken the exit.

## Bone Ossuary

- **Atmosphere:** Bone-white tunnels, fossil spirals, and relics left by earlier expeditions.
- **Playable rule:** Bone relics grant bonus shards, while fossil blocks may preserve food.
- **Future objective:** Reassemble a fossil guardian to make it reveal a hidden route.

## Storm Cavern

- **Atmosphere:** Blue-black rock, charged air, and lightning crawling over the ground.
- **Playable rule:** Spark tiles strike once and discharge, making every risky shortcut a choice.
- **Future objective:** Ground three lightning rods to make the final chamber safe.

## Rootbound Burrow

- **Atmosphere:** Tangled old roots, earthen rooms, sap glow, and broken wooden shrines.
- **Playable rule:** Root blocks provide three wood, making this the best cave for home expansion.
- **Future objective:** Free a trapped ancient tree without letting burrowing monsters reach its heart.

## Suggested rollout

1. **Frozen Depths:** Introduces one clear movement rule using the existing grid.
2. **Fungal Hollow:** Adds reusable tile effects and temporary status conditions.
3. **Flooded Grotto:** Builds on timed terrain and makes sprinting strategically important.
4. **Echo Labyrinth:** Expands visibility and enemy awareness after the core cave framework is established.
5. **The Nest:** Adds countdown objectives and a guardian encounter as a later-game challenge.
